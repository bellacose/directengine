A gang sheet is one long DTF print with many designs laid out on it, which you cut apart and press one by one. The printer prints your file exactly as sent, so most problems with gang sheets start in the file. This guide covers the settings DTF printers publish for gang sheet files and where they differ. Always check the file page of the shop that will print yours.

## The short version

- Size the file in inches to the exact sheet you ordered: width and length.
- Export one PNG with a transparent background.
- Make every design 300 DPI at the size it will print.
- Leave space between designs and keep artwork off the sheet's edge.
- Don't mirror anything.

## What printers publish

| Setting | [DTF Transfers Now](https://dtftransfersnow.com/pages/artwork-upload-requirements) | [T-shirt.ca](https://t-shirt.ca/dtf-knowledge-hub/dtf-artwork-requirements-and-file-formats/) | [Transfer Kingdom](https://transferkingdom.com/pages/art-file-requirements) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preferred format | PNG with a transparent background | Vector (SVG, AI, EPS, some PDF) preferred; PNG, JPG, PSD also accepted | AI, PDF, PSD, PNG, TIF or JPG |
| Resolution | 300 DPI at final print size | 300 DPI at actual print size | 150–900 DPI |
| Color mode | RGB, not CMYK | Not stated | CMYK |
| Sheet width | 22, 24 or 30 in, by product | Set in their builder | Up to 22 in, length chosen at checkout |
| Thin lines | Not stated | At least 0.5 mm | 2 pt lines, 2 pt gaps, 2 pt outlines |

The printers disagree on color mode: DTF Transfers Now asks for RGB, Transfer Kingdom for CMYK, which it also converts spot colors to. Send what your printer asks for.

## Size the canvas to the sheet

Set the canvas to the sheet's width and the length you ordered, in inches. DTF Transfers Now says to set the artboard to your product's sheet width and the length you ordered, that it does not resize anything, and that anything wider than the sheet "gets cut off" ([DTF Transfers Now](https://dtftransfersnow.com/pages/artwork-upload-requirements)).

## Resolution: 300 DPI at the printed size

Two of the three printers ask for 300 DPI at the size the design will print ([DTF Transfers Now](https://dtftransfersnow.com/pages/artwork-upload-requirements), [T-shirt.ca](https://t-shirt.ca/dtf-knowledge-hub/dtf-artwork-requirements-and-file-formats/)). Transfer Kingdom accepts 150–900 DPI ([Transfer Kingdom](https://transferkingdom.com/pages/art-file-requirements)).

Enlarging a small image doesn't add detail. T-shirt.ca says increasing the dimensions of a small, low-resolution image "will not improve its quality" ([T-shirt.ca](https://t-shirt.ca/dtf-knowledge-hub/dtf-artwork-requirements-and-file-formats/)), and DTF Transfers Now warns that images saved from social media or websites often lack enough pixels ([DTF Transfers Now](https://dtftransfersnow.com/pages/artwork-upload-requirements)).

To check a design: its width in pixels divided by its printed width in inches should be at least 300. A design printing 10 inches wide needs at least 3,000 pixels across.

## Transparent background, no white boxes

DTF printers print white ink, so a white background prints as a white block around your design ([DTF Transfers Now](https://dtftransfersnow.com/pages/artwork-upload-requirements)). Transfer Kingdom says any background you send will be printed ([Transfer Kingdom](https://transferkingdom.com/pages/art-file-requirements)). Remove backgrounds before exporting, and watch for screenshots, which carry their background with them ([DTF Transfers Now](https://dtftransfersnow.com/pages/artwork-upload-requirements)).

## Shadows, gradients and see-through areas

T-shirt.ca asks for fully opaque colors and says to avoid translucent, semi-transparent or low-opacity elements ([T-shirt.ca](https://t-shirt.ca/dtf-knowledge-hub/dtf-artwork-requirements-and-file-formats/)). Transfer Kingdom asks you to remove shadow and gradient effects, which it says "will not print as you see on the screen" ([Transfer Kingdom](https://transferkingdom.com/pages/art-file-requirements)).

Before exporting, look for soft drop shadows, glows, feathered edges and layers set below 100% opacity, and replace them with solid shapes or remove them.

## Thin lines and small text

Very fine details may not print or may not stick to the garment. Published minimums vary:

- T-shirt.ca: lines and small details at least 0.5 mm thick ([T-shirt.ca](https://t-shirt.ca/dtf-knowledge-hub/dtf-artwork-requirements-and-file-formats/)).
- Transfer Kingdom: 2 pt positive space, 2 pt negative space and 2 pt outlines ([Transfer Kingdom](https://transferkingdom.com/pages/art-file-requirements)).
- Kolormatrix says text around 0.10–0.12 inches tall (about 7–9 pt) and lines or gaps around 0.02–0.03 inches (about 1.5–2 pt) "may print in some cases", and calls gaps that small risky ([Kolormatrix](https://kolormatrix.com/blogs/news/how-small-is-too-small-for-dtf-printing-text-lines-detail-guidelines)).

If your design is near these sizes, thicken it or print it larger.

## Spacing and edges

DTF Transfers Now asks for at least 0.25 inches between designs when you lay out a sheet yourself, notes that its own gang sheet builder uses 0.2 inches, and says to keep artwork away from the sheet's edge ([DTF Transfers Now](https://dtftransfersnow.com/pages/artwork-upload-requirements)). The gap also gives you room to cut designs apart.

## Vector files and text

If you send vector files (AI, EPS, SVG, PDF), DTF Transfers Now asks you to convert all text to outlines first, and says any pixel images inside those files still need 300 DPI at final size ([DTF Transfers Now](https://dtftransfersnow.com/pages/artwork-upload-requirements)).

## Don't mirror

T-shirt.ca says not to mirror, flip or reverse the image before uploading ([T-shirt.ca](https://t-shirt.ca/dtf-knowledge-hub/dtf-artwork-requirements-and-file-formats/)). DTF Transfers Now says designs flipped by the customer "come out backward" ([DTF Transfers Now](https://dtftransfersnow.com/pages/artwork-upload-requirements)). Send the design the way it should read on the shirt.

## Common reasons a file gets rejected or reprinted

From [DTF Transfers Now](https://dtftransfersnow.com/pages/artwork-upload-requirements):

- Images too small for their printed size.
- White or screenshot backgrounds.
- Artwork wider than the sheet.
- Mirrored designs.
- No artwork attached to the order. They say such an order "does not enter production".
- Files uploaded blurry, misspelled or at the wrong size don't qualify for their free reprints.

## Checklist before you upload

1. Canvas is the exact sheet width and length, in inches.
2. Each design is 300 DPI at its printed size (or meets your printer's range).
3. Background is transparent; no white boxes.
4. No shadows, glows, gradients or partly transparent areas, unless your printer says they're fine.
5. Lines, gaps and text meet your printer's minimums.
6. At least 0.25 inches between designs and nothing at the edge.
7. Color mode matches what your printer asks for.
8. Nothing is mirrored.
9. Exported as one PNG, or as your printer's preferred format.

If a deadline is close and you're using a local DTF shop listed on this site, ask for its file rules before you send the file.
